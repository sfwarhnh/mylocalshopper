Shipping & Delivery Information

Weekly Dispatches Direct to Australia

Dispatch Schedule

All orders are packed fresh and shipped out every Saturday. Please ensure your order and payment are finalized before Friday midnight to join the current week's batch.

Minimum Weight Requirements

Minimum total order weight is 1kg to qualify for international dispatch to Australia.

How To Order?

Step 1 : Place Order

Browse our featured items or request custom products with minimum 1 kg.


Step 2 : Confirm & Pay

We calculate your total item cost and international shipping (31 AUD/kg, 7–10 working days). Secure payment is coordinated via WhatsApp using WISE Transfer.


Step 3 : Doorstep Delivery

Your items are handpicked fresh in Malaysia, packed securely, will be ship straight to your door!
